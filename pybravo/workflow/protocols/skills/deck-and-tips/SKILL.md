Keep each physical plate and rack as a distinct material. A tip box and the tips loaded in it have independent IDs: select the exact head–tip–box link, not a match by name or capacity. Check the head footprint and plate pitch, then propose only free deck positions that do not conflict with plate moves. A stack needs a verified bottom-to-top order; a buried plate must be destacked before access.

For no cross contamination, allocate fresh source-dedicated tips unless the scientist approves a narrower reuse path. A rack listed in the catalog is not loaded inventory; record fresh wells only after inspection. For 384-to-1536 quadrant stamping, each 384 source maps to its own reviewed A1/A2/B1/B2 anchor on each destination. Keep deck positions and inventory as proposals until the scientist confirms the physical deck.
